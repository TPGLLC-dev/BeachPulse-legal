The App combines third-party live data with a fixed table of information about each beach, and produces a ranking. Everything it shows is provided for planning purposes only and may be inaccurate, incomplete or out of date.

Entry fees, parking costs, whether dogs are permitted, whether a park closes when its lots fill, and opening hours are recorded from public sources, are not continuously verified, and may be wrong.
